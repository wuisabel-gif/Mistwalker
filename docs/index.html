The Build Log

Forging a blade into the fist.

Putting one sword in one hand turned into a tour of nearly every core Unity system : prefabs, bones, active-state, and a 100× scale trap. The screenshots below are the real debugging trail.

01

The invisible blade

Valid mesh, enabled renderer, a material, correct bounds : and nothing on screen. Only the transform gizmo floated in the dark. The first clue that something upstream was wrong.

02

The wrong hand

The warrior gripped… nothing. The PlayerController.weapon reference pointed at an axe under a disabled duplicate warrior : so everything attached there never rendered.

03

Mapping the rig

Editor diagnostics revealed two skeletons: a legacy Warrior_RightHand rig (disabled) and the live Warrior (1) with bones named hand.r. The blade belonged on the live one.

04

The 100× scale trap

Attached to the live hand at last : but the bones carried a lossyScale of 100. A localScale of 1 rendered a four-metre greatblade. localScale = 1 / parentLossyScale brought it to ~0.8 m.

05

Blade in hand

Normalised, gripped, and parented to the animating bone : the runic blade finally rides with the warrior through every frame of the walk. One last pass on the wrist angle and the saga begins.

Design Pillars

Four ideas hold the dark together.

The fog remembers

Terrain reveals as you advance and re-darkens what you abandon. Backtracking is never safe.

Honor, not score

Every draugr banished restores honor : the run currency, the health economy, and the win condition in one.

One good blade

No loadouts. A single runic sword. Mastery comes from spacing and timing, not gear.

“An object can be active itself and still vanish : because a parent up the chain is disabled. The whole branch goes dark.”
